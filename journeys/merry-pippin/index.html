Merry & Pippin

Merry & Pippin follows 12 mapped waypoints, from Parth Galen to Gates of Isengard.

Travellers across this route: Merry, Pippin (captives of Uglúk's Uruk-hai; then with Treebeard).

Follow this journey on the map

Dates reproduce the atlas chronology; some intermediate dates and stopping places are reconstructed. A dated waypoint does not mean every traveller was present. Approximate positions and route detours are illustrative, not canonical coordinates. Map positions are schematic reconstructions, not surveyed locations. The scale varies by region; map distances are estimates, not measured road lengths.
