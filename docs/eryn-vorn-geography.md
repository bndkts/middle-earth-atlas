# Eryn Vorn geography

The atlas is schematic: the coastline is not a surveyed or traced map. Preserve
these geographic relationships when editing the SVG or place coordinates:

- Eryn Vorn covers a cape extending south into Belegaer from western Minhiriath.
- The Baranduin reaches the sea northwest of the wooded cape. Its lower course
  passes north of the cape, leaving the woodland on the Minhiriath side.
- The Greyflood mouth and Lond Daer are farther southeast.

The earlier river drawing emptied into the inlet east of the existing cape.
Moving only the woodland east of that river left it inland. The correction
reroutes the lower Baranduin to the cape's northwestern shore and places the
forest on the existing promontory. The coastline itself is retained. The river
fill, estuary fade, river-label path, mouth marker, forest washes, trees and
forest marker must remain consistent. Tree visibility buckets must follow their
coordinates. The woodland and its shadow must remain on land.

References checked on 2026-09-13:

- [Encyclopedia of Arda: Eryn Vorn](https://www.glyphweb.com/arda/e/erynvorn.html)
  describes the cape extending south from the land east of the river mouth.
- [Tolkien Gateway: Eryn Vorn](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Eryn_Vorn)
  identifies the wooded cape and dark pine forest, citing *Unfinished Tales*
  and Tolkien's annotations for Pauline Baynes's map.
- [MERP regional map reproduced in a Cardolan review](https://intothedarkdimension.blogspot.com/2012/06/lost-realm-of-cardolan-review-of.html)
  provides a visual cross-check of the forested peninsula below the estuary.
  Its additional settlements and local lore are adaptation material and were
  not imported into the atlas.

Regression checks cover geographic ordering, alignment of the river and mouth,
land containment of the forest artwork, and tree visibility buckets. Browser
verification also checks the close-up view, selection and map centring.
