Minas Tirith

Gondor · city

The capital of Gondor since Osgiliath's ruin, a seven-tiered white city built into the root of Mount Mindolluin, crowned by the Citadel and the White Tower of Ecthelion. Besieged by the Witch-king's host at the Battle of the Pelennor Fields in T.A. 3019, it was relieved by the Rohirrim and Aragorn's fleet.
