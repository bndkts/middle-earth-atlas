Lothlórien

Lothlórien · realm

The hidden Elven realm between the Celebrant and the Anduin, ruled by Celeborn and Galadriel from Caras Galadhon beneath golden-leaved mallorn trees, protected by Galadriel's power from Sauron's sight. The Fellowship rested and was gifted here in T.A. 3019 after Gandalf's fall in Moria, before its passing overseas in the Fourth Age.
