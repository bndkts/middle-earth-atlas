Edoras

Rohan · town

Capital of Rohan since the days of King Brego, built upon a green hill where the White Mountains meet the plain at the mouth of Harrowdale. Crowned by the golden hall of Meduseld, it is the seat of King Théoden and later King Éomer, reached by a long stair and a guarded gate.
