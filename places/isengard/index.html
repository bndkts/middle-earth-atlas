Isengard

Rohan · fortress

A great circular fortress made by the Men of Númenor within the ring of Nan Curunír to guard the Gap of Rohan, later given over to the Wizard Saruman as its keeper. In T.A. 3019 Saruman's industry turned it into an armed camp, until the Ents stormed and flooded it after the Battle of the Hornburg.
