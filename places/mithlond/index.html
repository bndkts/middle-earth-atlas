Grey Havens

Lindon · port

The great Elven haven at the head of the Gulf of Lune, seat of Círdan the Shipwright, from which ships bearing the Three Rings' keepers and the Ring-bearers sail west over the Sea. It is the last Elvish city in Middle-earth and the point of departure for all who leave these shores for Aman.
