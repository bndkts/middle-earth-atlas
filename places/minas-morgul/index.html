Minas Morgul

Mordor · city

The Dead City: once Minas Ithil, Tower of the Rising Moon, built by Isildur as Gondor's eastern fortress in a valley of the Ephel Dúath facing Minas Anor. Taken by the Nazgûl in T.A. 2002, it became Minas Morgul, the Tower of Sorcery, seat of the Witch-king. Its walls glow with a corpse-pale light, its gate gapes like a mouth, and a bridge of hideous statues crosses the Morgulduin before it. Its host marched on Minas Tirith in T.A. 3019.

How to interpret this atlas

Chronicle

  1. SA 3320 — Isildur founds Minas Ithil as the eastern fortress of Gondor.
  2. SA 3429 — Sauron captures Minas Ithil; it is recovered after his fall.
  3. TA 2002 — The Nazgûl take Minas Ithil and its palantír; it becomes Minas Morgul.
  4. TA 2050 — King Eärnur rides to its gate to answer the Witch-king's challenge and is never seen again.
  5. TA 3019 — 10 March: the Morgul-host under the Witch-king marches out to besiege Minas Tirith.
