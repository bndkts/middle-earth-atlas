Dol Amroth

Gondor · town

The chief city of Belfalas, seat of its Princes who claim descent from the Elves of Edhellond, on a headland above the Bay of Belfalas. Prince Imrahil led its swan-knights to the defence of Minas Tirith in T.A. 3019, distinguishing himself in the Battle of the Pelennor Fields.
