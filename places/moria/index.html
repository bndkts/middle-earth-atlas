Moria

Misty Mountains · mine

The greatest mansion of the Dwarves, delved by Durin beneath the Misty Mountains. The Balrog killed Durin VI in T.A. 1980 and Náin I in 1981, after which the Dwarves fled. Balin briefly recolonised it in 2989–2994. It is a haunt of Orcs and worse when the Fellowship crosses it in 3019.

How to interpret this atlas

Chronicle

  1. YT 1 — c. Durin the Deathless founds Khazad-dûm.
  2. TA 1980 — The Dwarves awaken a Balrog; Durin VI is slain.
  3. TA 1981 — Náin I is slain; the surviving Dwarves flee Moria.
  4. TA 2989 — Balin leads Dwarves to recolonise Moria.
  5. TA 2994 — Balin's colony is destroyed by Orcs.
  6. TA 3019 — The Fellowship of the Ring passes through Moria; Gandalf falls fighting the Balrog.
